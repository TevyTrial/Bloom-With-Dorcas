# Bloom with Dorcas 🌱🎵

Bloom with Dorcas is a cozy third-person farming game that combines life-simulation mechanics with music-driven gameplay. Inspired by titles such as *Harvest Moon*, *Stardew Valley*, and *Mutazione*, the game focuses on calm exploration, farming routines, and a gentle sense of progression.

The player takes control of **Dorcas**, a sheep character based on a real handmade doll created by my aunt. This project was developed as part of a university coursework module and also acts as a side project that complements my Final Year Project, which explores interactive storytelling and character-driven design.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

The core gameplay loop centres around farming and resource management:

- Hoeing soil, planting seeds, watering crops, and harvesting
- Managing stamina and resting at the campsite
- Selling crops to earn coins and buy new seeds
- Drawing food from a magical pond to restore stamina
- Experiencing seasonal changes across Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter

Each crop contributes not only to the economy, but also to the **musical atmosphere** of the farm.

---

## 🎵 Music-Based Farming System

One of the key features of Bloom with Dorcas is its adaptive music system.

- Each season has its own background song
- Mature crops trigger individual instrument tracks
- As more crops grow, the music gradually builds into a full seasonal composition
- Audio is spatialised in 3D space, allowing players to hear instruments as they move around the farm

This system encourages players to experiment with planting different crops and rewards patience with a calming, layered soundscape.

---

## 🌦 Time & Seasons

- The in-game year is divided into 4 seasons
- Each season lasts 7 in-game days
- Weather and environment visuals change with time progression
- Trees and lighting update dynamically to reflect the current season and time of day

These systems help reinforce immersion while keeping gameplay sessions short and accessible.

## 🧍 Third-Person Character Controller

- Custom third-person controller developed in Unity
- Right-click camera orbit with zoom control
- Reset camera shortcut for accessibility
- Character model sculpted in **Nomad Sculpt**
- Rigging and animations created using **Mixamo**

## 🧠 AI & Tools Used

This project also served as an opportunity to experiment with modern AI-assisted tools:

- **Suno AI** – seasonal music generation  
- **Voice.ai** – instrument stem separation  
- **Gemini (Nano-banana)** – UI and visual asset exploration  
- **Claude 4.5** – coding assistance for audio synchronization  
- **ChatGPT** – wording refinement and proofreading  
- **Canva** – UI layout experimentation  

All gameplay design, system integration, and implementation decisions were made by me. AI tools were used to support asset creation, debugging, and refinement.
